# 🍔 QuickBite Recovery Dashboard  

### 🚀 *A Data-Driven Strategy to Rebuild Trust, Retention & Operational Excellence after a Brand Crisis*

---

## 📖 Project Overview  
**QuickBite Express**, a food-tech startup based in Bengaluru, faced a severe brand crisis in **June 2025** due to food safety violations and delivery outages.  
This Power BI dashboard analyzes **Pre-Crisis (Jan–May 2025)** vs **Crisis (Jun–Sep 2025)** performance to uncover the impact and design recovery strategies.

The project covers:  
- Customer churn and sentiment analysis  
- Restaurant performance and partner risk  
- Delivery efficiency and SLA tracking  
- Strategy recommendations for recovery

## 📊 Dashboard Pages  

| Page | Focus | Key Insights |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **1️⃣ Primary Analysis (Q1–Q9)** | Crisis impact across orders, revenue, ratings, cancellations | Orders ↓69%, Revenue ↓71%, Avg Rating ↓2 points |
| **2️⃣ High-Value Customer Impact (Q10)** | Spending and loyalty decline among top 5% customers | Loyal users’ AOV ↑18%, but 4.8K high-value users lapsed |
| **3️⃣ Executive Summary** | Leadership snapshot of performance | Severe trust loss, revenue collapse, early signs of stabilization |
| **4️⃣ Customer Engagement** | Churn, loyalty attrition, and re-engagement trends | 84% loyalty attrition; referral & organic users churned fastest |
| **5️⃣ Restaurant Retention** | Partner churn and cuisine performance | Cloud kitchens lost 70%+ revenue; dine-in restaurants more resilient |
| **6️⃣ Delivery Operations** | SLA, delay, and efficiency patterns | On-time % fell to 36.5%; avg delay +12 mins; uniform network issues |
| **7️⃣ Sentiment & Feedback** | Customer emotion and review insights | Sentiment flipped from +0.8 → -0.3; top issues: safety & food quality |

---

## 🧠 Insights Summary  

> “QuickBite’s challenge wasn’t just operational — it was emotional.  
> Customers lost trust faster than they stopped ordering. Recovery depends on restoring confidence, not just offering discounts.”

**Key Findings**
- ⚠️ 69% order and 71% revenue decline during crisis  
- 💔 Avg rating dropped from 4.6 → 2.3; sentiment from 0.8 → -0.3  
- 👥 84% loyalty attrition; 4,800+ high-value users inactive  
- 🍽️ Cloud kitchens hit hardest; restaurant partners more resilient  
- 🚴 On-time delivery fell to 36%; delay increased by 12 minutes

## 🎯 Recovery Strategy Framework  

**1. SafeBite Guarantee (Trust Rebuild)**  
✅ Visible hygiene & food safety assurance  
✅ “Hygiene Verified” restaurant tags  
✅ Transparent audits & partner QA  

**2. Loyalty Comeback Campaign**  
🎁 Cashback + personalized messages to 4.8K high-value users  
💬 “We’ve earned back your trust — let’s serve you safely again.”  

**3. Operational Excellence**  
📡 Real-time SLA monitoring dashboards  
🕒 City-level targets to raise on-time % from 36% → 60%  

**4. Sentiment Recovery Loop**  
💌 Proactive follow-up on negative reviews  
📈 Weekly keyword sentiment monitoring
